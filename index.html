One war, many worlds

Each world here is a complete, original setting for a war between rulers: its land, its economy, its rules of battle, every unit with what it beats and what kills it, the armies that work — and one full war, start to finish. The worlds look nothing alike. The mistakes that lose them are the same.

The eight lessons

Every first chronicle turns on the same eight blunders. Here is what each one is called in each world. The second chronicles cover eight more, and the Beat Library has the rest.

Beyond the eight

Wars turn on more than eight mistakes. The Beat Library covers twenty-nine moments, from spies in the capital and allies already inside your walls to the forbidden weapon and the last-day land grab, each translated into every world.

How to read a world page

World & economy

The map, the people, what they produce, what armies cost to keep, and why conquered places turn against you.

Rules of battle

The dozen rules that create every surprise: hit classes, formation limits, digging in, range, who can take ground, low flyers, escorts, stealth, transports, bases.

Roster & matrix

Every unit rated 0–5 against every hit class. The matrix shows at a glance what to bring against what. "Dies to" is computed, not written by hand.

Synergies

Pairings that cover each other's blind spots.

Compositions

Ten complete armies per world for free-for-all and team play, with how they win and how they die.

Two chronicles

Two whole wars per world, told in that world's own voice. The first is a lone aggressor against an alliance; the second is alliance against alliance, with betrayal. Each ends with why the loser lost.
