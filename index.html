Three worlds, one academy story

Where wars are learned

In each of these worlds, young commanders from places that distrust each other are sent to the same academy to learn to fight full-scale wars inside a simulation. In two of the worlds the simulated wars decide real borders. In the third they decide championships. Every world has its own map, economy, rules of battle, units with counters, the armies that work, a cast, and two complete wars told start to finish.

The worlds

Pick a world. Each page stands on its own.

Near future · Earth

Meridian 2076

After one war nearly destroyed them, twelve successor states agreed to settle disputes in a binding simulation. They all send their cadets to the one neutral academy that holds it.
Far future · Colony world

Corvane

On a half-terraformed planet, the colony ship's mind controls the air everyone breathes. It forbids real war, so the rival charters fight inside it and send their children aboard to learn how.
Present-day · Sport

Grand Theatre

In a peaceful world, simulated war is the biggest sport on the planet. One island academy is licensed to train its future stars, and every federation sends its prodigies there.

How to read a world page

The academy

Why the academy exists, why rivals attend it, how its years and exams work, and the cadets who could carry a book.

World, cast & map

The land and the people, six leaders built around six ways of playing a war, and a map with the first war's turning points drawn on it.

Economy & rules

What a nation produces, what armies cost to keep, why conquests rebel, and the dozen rules that create every surprise.

Roster & matrix

Every unit rated 0–5 against every hit class. "Dies to" is computed from the ratings, not written by hand.

Synergies & compositions

Pairings that cover each other's blind spots, and ten complete armies for free-for-all and team play.

Two chronicles

Two whole wars per world. The first is a lone aggressor against an alliance; the second is alliance against alliance, with betrayal. Each ends with why the loser lost.
